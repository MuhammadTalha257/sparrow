Meet Sparrow

A friendly little helper that remembers your meetings, tasks and files, tells you your day, and does things when you just say “Sparrow…”. Free, private and works offline.

No account · No subscription · Your data stays on your device

Mac

  2. Drag Sparrow to Applications
  3. First open: System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway (once)

What Sparrow does

Everything below works without any AI key. Add a free AI any time for open questions.

🗣️ Hands-free — “Sparrow, open Chrome”, no button needed
⏰ Reminders that talk — “Talha, you have a meeting in 5 minutes”
🔁 Repeating reminders & snooze — “every day at 8pm take medicine”
☀️ Morning briefing — weather, meetings and today's tasks
🌙 Evening check-in — tick what's done, move the rest
🕌 Prayer times — worked out offline, with a reminder
💧 Habits — water, medicine, anything you track
🧠 Private memory — “which file did I send on 12 Sept?”
📄 Ask your documents — drop a PDF, Word or Excel file and ask
🧾 Quotes & invoices — “quote for Ali, 3 hours at £40” → PDF
⏱️ Time tracking & expenses — straight into invoices
🎤 Meeting notes — written offline, action items become tasks
📑 PDF tools — merge, photos to PDF, keep pages, rotate
📁 Files — find by voice, tidy Downloads, folder alerts (computer)
📋 Clipboard history & snippets
🎵 Music — “play Tum Hi Ho on Spotify”
🌍 English, اردو, हिन्दी, العربية
🤖 Any AI you like — Ollama (free), Groq, Gemini, ChatGPT, Claude, Grok, DeepSeek, Mistral, Perplexity
