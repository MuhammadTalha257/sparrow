Meet Sparrow

A friendly little AI helper that keeps your meetings, tasks and reminders — and opens your apps when you ask. Free.

Get Sparrow
Free · No account · Your data stays on your device
💻

Mac

  1. Download and open Sparrow.dmg
  2. Drag Sparrow to Applications
  3. First open: System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway (once)
Download for Mac
🤖

Android

  1. Download Sparrow.apk
  2. Open it — allow “Install unknown apps” if asked
  3. Open Sparrow 🐦
Download for Android
📱

iPhone & iPad

  1. Open the app link in Safari
  2. Tap Share → Add to Home Screen
  3. Open Sparrow from your home screen
Open the app
🪟

Windows

  1. Open the app link in Edge or Chrome
  2. Click the Install icon in the address bar
  3. Sparrow gets its own window & Start menu icon
